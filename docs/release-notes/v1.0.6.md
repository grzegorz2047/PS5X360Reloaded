## TrinityX360 v1.0.6

The app has a new name, **TrinityX360** (until v1.0.5 it was called PS5X360Reloaded): the same app with new art. The home screen shows `TrinityX360 v1.0.6`. It replaces v1.0.5 rather than adding a second app. See "Upgrading from v1.0.5" at the end before you update, and back up your settings first.

**How to read the status tags below:**
- `[console]`: confirmed on a PS5.
- `[host tests]`: verified by tests on a computer (run in CI), not confirmed on the console.

TrinityX360 is based on [PS5X360](https://github.com/BrinooTk/PS5X360) by BrinooTk and on
[Xenia Canary](https://github.com/xenia-canary/xenia-canary). Trinity is the name of the Xbox 360 S motherboard.

### New name: TrinityX360
- **Only the name and the art changed**: the title id stays `PPSA83600` and the data folder `/data/homebrew/PPSA83600`, so v1.0.6 installs over v1.0.5 and the console keeps one tile. Forwarder tiles keep working.
- **Saves on other drives stay where they are**: the folder on each M.2 or USB drive keeps its name, `<drive>/PS5X360Reloaded/saves`. Do not rename it.
- **The PS5 may keep the old name and icon** after the update, because it stores them when an app is first installed. To refresh them, start the PS5 in Safe Mode and choose "Rebuild Database": it keeps your games, apps and saved data. Deleting the app and installing it again also works, but it erases the app's internal storage, including saves kept in "Internal storage"; back them up first (see "Upgrading from v1.0.5").

### Games
- **GTA IV: far fewer pauses while driving** (fixes 150–150f) `[console, at 1x]`: the short pauses that came more and more often while you drove through the city are gone in our test runs at 1x. The longest pause went from 356–660 ms to 29–85 ms, at 43–57 FPS. At 2x it was tested only over a few minutes (see Known limitations).
- **GTA IV: draw distance patches** (issue #52) `[console]`: the Patches tab has two optional patches, "Draw Distance +25%" and "Draw Distance +50%". They are off by default; turn on only one. Buildings, trees, people and vehicles stay detailed farther away. The cost is about 15% or 28% of the frame rate (open waterfront view: 52–55 FPS off, 46 at +25%, 38–40 at +50%).
- **GTA V: the Install disc works** (issue #51; fixes 148, 148b) `[console]`: "Disc 1 (Install)" no longer drops you back to the library after a second, and the game installs its data. The two No-Intro disc images are recognised as Disc 1 and Disc 2 of one game.
- **GTA V: a stopped install is explained** (issue #51; fixes 148e, 148f, 148h) `[console]`: if an install was stopped part-way, the game no longer hangs on a black screen. The app shows "Install data incomplete" with steps to follow, and the game's DLCs tab marks the package "incomplete: X of Y" so you can remove it and install it again.
- **GTA V: prologue audio freeze patch** (fix 155c) `[console]`: a built-in patch, "Audio freeze fix (prologue)", is on by default and gets the game past the prologue freeze. Patch files you put in `/data/homebrew/PPSA83600/assets/patches/` (as the Patches tab says) are now loaded `[host tests]`.
- **GTA V: new recommended settings** `[console]`: "Image readback by the game" off and "Optimized video memory" on. After loading, the game holds a steady 30 FPS, its own cap, and the picture no longer darkens.
- **Dance Central 3 starts and plays** (issue #50; fixes 147, 147b, 147c) `[console]`: it no longer stops on a black screen after the logo or on its loading screen. On the first start it shows "Music keys needed" with a QR code, so you can upload the game's music keys from your phone. After a restart the game plays.
- **Virtua Tennis 3 no longer freezes during a replay** (issue #61; fix 151) `[console]`: about 25 minutes over two sessions with every replay watched, and no freeze.
- **FIFA Street: the pitch is no longer black** (issue #62) `[console]`: its recommended settings now use full image readback.
- **Games that list the storage devices** (for example Just Cause 2) no longer think there is no room to save (issue #47; fix 144) `[host tests]`.

### Kinect
- **Kinect Sports: Season Two: darts loads and plays** (issue #63; fixes 156b, 156d–156g, 156j, 156p) `[console: darts]`: darts no longer stays on its loading screen. Some stutter remains, and the other sports are not confirmed yet.
- **Fighters Uncaged gets past player identification** (issue #67; fixes 156i, 156l, 156n) `[console]`: it no longer waits forever, and it reaches Training with the signed-in profile at about 60 FPS.
- **Zumba Fitness Core finds the camera** (issue #63; fixes 156, 156q) `[console]`: it no longer says it cannot find the camera, but it still stops at "Searching for player" (see Known limitations). Dragon Ball Z for Kinect still works with the updated player identification.
- **Kinect games start at 1x; camera pictures are correct at 2x** (fixes 162a, 162c, 162d, 162f) `[console]`: under "Automatic", every game that needs Kinect starts at 1x. This avoids the out-of-memory stutter and broken replays Kinect Sports had at 2x. If you pick 2x yourself, the Kinect camera pictures (in Kinect Sports, the stadium screen and the replay panel) are now drawn correctly instead of as green blocks or black. Kinect Sports at 1x: 45–59 FPS.
- **Kinect Sports replays without stutter** (issue #55; fixes 150d, 150d3) `[console]`: at 1x, which "Automatic" uses for it, the game no longer stutters while it records and saves replays.
- **Kinect games at 2x no longer drop to 0–2 FPS after a few minutes** (issues #64, #65; fix 154) `[console: Kinect Sports]`: this slowdown no longer happens; Kinect Sports held 59–60 FPS through 4–5 minute test runs. Memory at 2x is still tight (see Known limitations).
- **The virtual Kinect is reported ready earlier, and its tilt angle is answered** (issues #66, #63; fixes 156, 156c, 156f) `[host tests]`: Kinect games that check for the sensor while loading (Harry Potter Kinect, Nike+ Kinect Training) or move its tilt on the start screen (Kinect Adventures) should get further.

### Picture and performance
- **"Automatic" resolution scale, the new default** (fix 163) `[console]`: "Resolution scale (test)" has a new choice, "Automatic (2x while the game keeps 30 fps)". Games run at 2x, which is sharper, while they keep 30 FPS and have enough memory. A game that doesn't shows "This game runs better at 1x" and runs at 1x from its next start. Resetting the game's own settings tries 2x again. GTA IV under "Automatic" runs at 2x at 43–56 FPS.
- **GTA V, Battlefield 3, Tekken Tag Tournament 2 and Red Dead Redemption start at 1x under "Automatic"** (fix 162h) `[console: GTA V]`: they came close to the memory limit at 2x. Picking 2x yourself still works.
- **Anti-aliasing** (issue #43; fixes 137, 137b, 145) `[console]`: a new "Anti-aliasing" option smooths jagged edges with FXAA, FXAA Extreme or SMAA 1x. It is set per game, can be changed live from the guide, and is off by default.
- **"Color preset" and "Overscan crop"** (issue #44; fixes 141, 141b) `[console]`: Off, OLED Deep Black, Vibrant or Clean Neutral, and a crop of 0–3% from each edge to suit your TV. Both are off by default.
- **"Emulated console resolution" now reaches the game** (issue #45; fixes 140, 140b, 140c) `[console]`: the choices are 1080p, the new 900p, 720p and 480p widescreen; 540p tells the game 720p. A change applies from the next game start. Minecraft no longer crashes at 480p.
- **"Frame rate limit (screen)"** (issues #48, #49; fixes 146–146d) `[console]`: Automatic, No limit, 30, 40 or 60. It caps how often the TV gets a new picture, for steadier motion, without slowing the game itself.
- **No more short screen freezes every ~30 seconds** (issue #48; fixes 152, 153) `[console]`: in long sessions the picture no longer stands still for a moment. Some games, for example Modern Warfare 2, also start faster.
- **GTA IV and Red Dead Redemption: a little faster by default** (issues #24, #25; fix 134e) `[console]`: higher and steadier frame rates. GTA IV went from 43 to 45.5 FPS, and Red Dead Redemption's lowest frame rate went from 28 to 30.
- **Game cache files on the M.2 extended storage** (fixes 150c, 150e) `[console]`: if you have an M.2 drive, games that keep rebuilding cache files while you play use it instead of the console's internal storage.

### Settings and the guide
- **A new settings menu** (issue #57; fixes 149, 149b) `[console]`: settings are now a menu with several levels. "Picture and smoothness" only changes the finished picture. "Emulation (advanced)" changes how the game runs and shows a warning. There are also "Game settings" and "Library and saves" entries. Option names are clearer, and in the guide a help card appears beside the focused option. The L1/R1 tabs in the settings and "Next subject" in the game sheet are gone.
- **Easier to read on a TV** (issue #57; fixes 149c, 149d) `[console]`: text has more contrast and the button hints are larger. The guide's help card no longer covers the motion or music-keys panels.
- **A change made in the guide stays with the running game** (issue #46; fix 143) `[console]`: an option you change in the in-game guide (for example "Emulated video rate" or anisotropic filtering) now applies only to the game you are playing, not to every game.

### Library and home screen
- **New name: TrinityX360, with new art** (fix 199) `[console]`: the new name appears on the PS5 home screen, in the guide header and on the phone and web pages. It is the same app with the same title ID, PPSA83600.
- **New icons on the app's home screen** (fix 164) `[console]`: the "Trinity rings" icons, in both the dark and the light theme.
- **A folder with one multi-disc game is one game** (fix 159) `[console]`: a folder that holds the discs of one game (Battlefield 3, Final Fantasy XIII, GTA V) now shows as that game instead of a group of separate tiles.
- **Your settings are kept in the data folder** (fix 165) `[console]`: settings, per-game settings, the recent list, game folders, covers and your other personal files now live in `/data/homebrew/PPSA83600`. They survive a reinstall, and you can reach them over FTP.

### Known limitations
- Everything listed for v1.0.5 still applies unless it is fixed above. Still open from v1.0.5: Virtua Tennis 3 players are invisible in some matches (issue #17), Battlefield 3 shows brief black squares near smoke in the carrier-deck intro, and the Kinect Sports replay preview with the phone's camera picture is not smooth.
- **Zumba Fitness Core** still stops at "Searching for player" (issue #63). A fix is in testing for the next release.
- **Kinect Sports: Season Two**: darts loads and plays, but some stutter remains. The other sports are not confirmed on the console yet (issue #63).
- **Harry Potter Kinect, Nike+ Kinect Training and Kinect Adventures**: the changes for these games are tested only on a computer, not on the console (issues #66, #63).
- **Kinect games at 2x chosen by hand**: the camera pictures are correct, but memory is tight, so stutter or replay artifacts can appear after a while. "Automatic", the default, starts these games at 1x. A real fix for 2x memory is planned for the next release.
- **GTA IV at 2x**: a thin 1-pixel line around the minimap and a thin seam in the picture. Both are cosmetic, and a fix is planned. To avoid them, set the game's own "Resolution scale (test)" to "1x (720p, native)".
- **GTA IV at 2x keeps its cache files on the drive**, because the memory goes to the sharper picture. The driving pauses could come back in long sessions at 2x, where only a few minutes were measured. At 1x the cache is in memory.
- **"Automatic" changes the scale only from a game's next start**: a game that struggles at 2x finishes the current session at 2x.
- **Red Dead Redemption at 2x** chosen by hand ran at about 20–30 FPS in v1.0.5. "Automatic" starts it at 1x.
- **"Frame rate limit (screen)" at 40** needs a 120 Hz output. This release does not ask the PS5 for 120 Hz, so 40 is not applied on a 60 Hz output (issue #49).
- **Dance Central 3** needs you to upload the game's music keys, which are not included. The game needs a restart after the upload.
- **GTA V install speed**: after about 1.5 GB has been written in one app session, the PS5 slows the app's drive writes to about 2 MB/s, so the rest of the install is slow. Restarting the app brings the full speed back.
- **A game's own saved setting still wins over the new recommended settings**: if you earlier set FIFA Street's or GTA V's own "Image readback by the game", that value is kept. Reset that game's own settings to use the recommended one.
- **Minor**: the launcher can pause for about 2 s while it scans the library at start.
- **Minor, found by reading the code**: when a game with its own start options restarts the app into itself, the recent list can shrink to that one game. This can also change the disc remembered for a multi-disc game.

### Upgrading from v1.0.5
- **Install it over v1.0.5 as before.** Use the whole package (`PPSA83600.ffpkg`, `.ffpfsc` or `.zip`, only one of them), never `eboot.bin` alone. The title ID (PPSA83600) and the data folder (`/data/homebrew/PPSA83600`) are the same, and its `contentVersion` is `01.006.000`, so the console sees v1.0.5 as an older install.
- **Back up your settings first.** A reinstall can clear the app's internal storage before v1.0.6 starts for the first time, and then your v1.0.5 settings cannot be copied. While v1.0.5 is open, copy them over FTP from `/mnt/sandbox/PPSA83600_000/download0/xbox360ps5/` (the `_000` part can differ on your console).
- **Your settings move on their own if they are still there.** On the first start, v1.0.6 copies these once from the internal storage to `/data/homebrew/PPSA83600`: settings, per-game settings, profile, recent list, game folders, covers, avatars, patch choices, frame generation settings and a few other files. It keeps the old copies and never replaces a file that is already in the data folder. The one exception is the frame generation settings: the copy from the internal storage is used, and the file that was already in the data folder is kept beside it with `.before-move` added to its name.
- **The new name changes nothing that identifies your data.** Saves and game caches on other drives stay in their folders named `PS5X360Reloaded` (for example `/mnt/ext1/xbox360/PS5X360Reloaded/saves`). Do not rename them. Saves are not moved by this release.
- **Resolution scale**: a general "Resolution scale (test)" of 1x becomes "Automatic" once, even if you chose 1x yourself. Most games now start at 2x and switch to 1x from their next start if 2x does not suit them. A game where you chose 1x or 2x in its own settings keeps your choice. For 1x everywhere, set "Resolution scale (test)" back to "1x (720p, native)" in the general settings.
- **Settings pages**: the old Video and Performance pages are now "Picture and smoothness" and "Emulation (advanced)". Older instructions that say "Under Video..." point to these groups.
- **Phone camera**: a certificate made before v1.0.6 keeps the old name until you make it again. The instructions name both.
